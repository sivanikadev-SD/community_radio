Frequently Asked Questions

Can I record my live shows?

Yes, Pro plans include automatic cloud recording and archiving for all live broadcasts, allowing you to easily publish them later.

What percentage do you take from donations?

SonicStream takes 0% commission for Pro users. Free users incur a 5% fee. The only other fees are standard payment processing fees from our partners.

Do I need special hardware to broadcast?

No, you can start broadcasting directly from your web browser. However, for the best audio quality, we recommend using a DJ controller and a dedicated audio interface.
