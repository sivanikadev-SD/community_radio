The Architecture
of Sound

SonicStream was founded by a collective of DJs and engineers with a single mission: to create a platform where global audio can thrive without technical barriers.

From Warehouse to Worldwide

In 2021, we started as a small internal tool for a pirate radio station in East London. We quickly realized that the challenges of low-latency streaming and fair artist compensation were universal.

Today, SonicStream powers over 150 independent stations across 40 countries, processing millions in listener donations and providing a stage for thousands of creative voices.
